# Test Case: Student Views Exam Results

**Test Case ID:** TC-STUDENT-001
**Test Suite:** Student: Dashboard & Analytics

**Feature:** View Exam Results
**Test Objective:** Verify that a student can view their detailed results and performance analytics for a completed and finalized exam.

---

### Pre-conditions
*   The user must be logged in as a Student.
*   The student must have a completed exam that has been processed and finalized by an instructor.
*   The student has received a notification that their results are available.

---

### Test Steps
1.  Log in to the application as a student.
2.  From the student dashboard, locate the finalized exam in the list of completed exams.
3.  Click on the exam title or a "View Results" button associated with it.

---

### Expected Result
*   The user is navigated to the detailed results page for that exam.
*   The page correctly displays:
    *   The student's overall score (e.g., 85/100).
    *   A topic-wise breakdown of performance (e.g., Subject A: 9/10, Subject B: 7/10).
    *   Any personalized feedback left by the instructor.
*   All data on the page matches the records in the database.
